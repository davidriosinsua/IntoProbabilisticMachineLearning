About the course

Welcome to Into Probabilistic Machine Learning. This course provides an introduction to probabilistic approaches to machine learning, connecting Bayesian modelling, computation, regression, neural networks and probabilistic graphical models.

The course consists of six lessons. Each lesson includes a slide deck and a ZIP archive containing the R Markdown files, documents and datasets used in the practical work.
